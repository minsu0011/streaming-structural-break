# Scale-down: evidence at three different levels

This research synthesis combines fixed-generator channel diagnostics with previously frozen model results. It introduces no new model or selection decision.

1. **Initialization/calibration:** In the 1,000 original calibration H samples per background, median energy-channel H SD is approximately 1.434 (Gaussian), 1.435 (AR), 2.889 (ARCH) and 3.175 (heavy-tail ARCH). The corresponding median exact negative current-energy floors are -0.710, -0.709, -0.380 and -0.354. The median top-one-percent share of centered energy sum-of-squares is 0.288, 0.289, 0.731 and 0.753. These are descriptive across-H summaries, not fitted-model causal effects.

2. **Current channels:** On the separate, already fixed partition-3 counterexample pairs, both clipped current channels retain a negative average response to a variance decrease. At variance factor 0.7, the paired 512-point raw-energy mean differences are Gaussian -0.215712, AR -0.213649, ARCH -0.109792 and heavy-tail ARCH -0.102047. At factor 0.5 they are -0.359543, -0.356095, -0.185309 and -0.171884. Thus the raw-energy response is approximately halved in the ARCH/tail backgrounds under this normalization. However the forecast channel does respond: at factor 0.7 its mean differences are -0.181304 in ARCH and -0.166173 in heavy-tail ARCH; at factor 0.5 they are -0.326083 and -0.294529. Every one of the 512 paired ARCH/tail trajectories has a negative mean difference for both channels at these horizons. The registered conditional paired intervals exclude zero for all these aggregate means. This rules out a claim that both basic channels simply lack a signed scale-down response in these cases. It does not prove an equally useful downstream statistic or imply a linear tree response.

3. **Learned detection:** Despite those channel responses, J102 has cross-background synthetic TS-AUC 0.475148/0.413566 at factor 0.7 in ARCH/heavy-tail ARCH, while its Gaussian/AR values are 0.847659/0.850647. The 80/20 blend is lower still in these two difficult cells. B104 improves the ARCH cell to 0.536460 but does not meet the real DEV noninferiority gate. Separately, removal of GLR memory reduces the long-lived response to a single outlier. These observations point to the downstream mixture of summaries, long memory and learned decision boundaries as a worthwhile next research target; they do not isolate one responsible tree feature or establish a successful repair.

The moment calculation in `NEXT2_SYNTHETIC_MOMENT_INTERPRETATION.md` explains why the limiting ARCH/tail backgrounds can have finite variance but no finite fourth moment. The H diagnostic uses partition 0, while the response and model counterexamples use partition 3. They are intentionally not treated as matched samples or combined into a regression. The channel response bootstrap resamples whole matched control/intervention seed pairs and is conditional on the fixed generator; the H quantiles are not confidence intervals. No result here estimates competition-scale mechanism frequency beyond the separately reported DEV signature coverage.
